#  Superstore Excel Business Intelligence Dashboard

This is my first Data Analytics portfolio project. In this project, I cleaned, modeled, and analyzed a retail dataset consisting of **5,900+ rows** of transactional data to build a fully interactive Business Intelligence dashboard.

##  Problem Statement
"The management of a Superstore wants to analyze their sales and profit performance for 2019 and 2020. However, the raw data is very large (5,900+ rows) and stored in a single flat Excel sheet. Running heavy analysis directly on this sheet slows down Excel. To solve this, a fast and interactive dashboard is needed to track monthly sales trends, find top-selling categories, locate high-performing states, and monitor product returns without dropping Excel’s performance."

---

##  Research Questions
- What are the monthly sales and profit growth trends across 2019 and 2020?
- Which product category (Furniture, Office Supplies, or Technology) generates the highest net profit, and which operates on the lowest profit margin?
- Who are the top 10 high-value customers, and which are the top 10 revenue-generating states?
- What is the impact of product return rates and distribution segments on overall business profitability?

---

##  Project Objectives
- **Data Cleaning:** Used Power Query to remove data errors, handle duplicates, and fix text/date formatting.
- **Performance Optimization:** Loaded the large dataset into Excel's native
- **Data Model (Power Pivot)** to keep the workbook fast, light, and optimized.
- **Calculations:** Wrote custom DAX formulas to dynamically calculate critical business metrics like Total Orders, Profit Margin %, Return Rate, and Average Order Value (AOV).
- **Dashboard Design:** Created a clean, cohesive, and user-friendly dashboard interface using standard charts and synchronized **Slicers (Region & Year)** for easy, real-time filtering.

---

##  Project Goals
- Help managers see clear monthly sales trends to plan stock and inventory properly.
- Highlight operational issues, like the 10% return rate, so the team can take data-driven action.
- Allow regional teams to filter data by year and region to understand local sales performance better.

---

##  Key Insights & Findings
- **Total Revenue:** ₹1,566K 
- **Total Net Profit:** ₹175K with an overall **Profit Margin of 11.19%**.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹521.41 across **3,003 total orders**.
- **Geographic Leader:** **California**  is the highest revenue-generating state, and a customer spends an average of ₹521 per order.

---

##  How to View & Use the Project
1. Download the `Superstore_BI_Dashboard.xlsx` file from this repository.
2. Open the file using **Microsoft Excel** (Excel 2016 or newer recommended for full Data Model compatibility).
3. Interact with the dashboard by clicking the **Region** or **Year** slicer buttons at the top right to filter all data points dynamically.
